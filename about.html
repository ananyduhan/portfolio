About

I'm studying information technology at Macquarie University in Sydney, majoring in AI. Most of what I build sits close to the model: quantized inference on phones, interpretability experiments on small transformers, and evaluation harnesses that try to say something more useful than a single benchmark score.

I care about measurement. It's easy to ship something that looks like it works — a demo, a fine-tune, a leaderboard number. It's harder to show what actually changed and what it cost. That gap is what most of my projects are about.

Outside that, I build and fly FPV drones, run, lift, and follow Formula 1 more closely than is strictly reasonable.
